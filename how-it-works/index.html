How it works

From sign-up to touchdown, in four steps.

You do a few simple things from home. We take care of the rest, and tell you in the app at every step.

01

Create your account

Enter your email and we send you a code. Type it in and you’re in. There are no passwords to remember.

  • Tell us your full name exactly as it’s on your passport. It goes on your airport name sign and your bookings.
  • You can switch the app to हिंदी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ or 中文 at any time.
02

Share your flight

Add your flight number and when you land. Your home screen turns into a boarding pass that counts down to the day.

  • We check your flight before you land and adjust your pickup if it’s delayed.
  • You can change the details any time until your stay is confirmed.
03

Choose your first stay and ride

Pick where you’d like to stay and for how long, then how you’d like to get there. Send us your request and we book it.

  • You see the cancellation terms and the amounts before you confirm anything.
  • It only shows as confirmed once it’s really booked, and we tell you the moment it is.

See the stay and ride options

Stay Hostel bed, homestay or motel From a few days up to four weeks
Ride Prepaid transfer, your own Uber, or a route guide Prepaid transfers priced before you pay
Status Confirmed before you fly Or we refund our fee
04

Land and be met

Someone is waiting at International Arrivals with a sign that has your name on it. You get a transport card and a local SIM, and a ride to your stay.

  • With Settle, we help you find a longer-term room: you send us listings, and we view them on video.
  • Our team is a message away in the app: for 2 weeks with Arrive, until you move in with Settle.

The whole journey

Before you fly, on the day, and after you land.

Before you fly

  • Create your account
  • Add your flight
  • Choose your stay and ride
  • We confirm your stay in the app
  • Start your renter pack (Settle)

On the day

  • We keep an eye on your flight
  • Someone meets you with your name
  • Transport card and local SIM
  • A ride to your first stay

After you land

  • Check in to your first stay
  • Send us rooms you like
  • We view them and check the landlord
  • Chat with the team whenever you need
