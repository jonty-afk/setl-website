Pricing

Clear prices, before you fly.

One package fee to SETL. Your stay and your ride are paid at cost, and the app shows every amount before you confirm anything.

One fee

One fee, whichever ride you choose.

A one-off fee per student. The same price in every city.

SETL fee · launch price

$199one-off

From landing to finding your own room. A launch price for our first students.

  • Someone waiting at the airport with your name
  • Welcome pack: your city’s bus card with $10 on it, and a NZ SIM
  • Your first stay booked before you fly, or your fee back
  • Help finding a permanent room: 2 listing checks and 1 room viewing included. An extra listing check is NZ$10 and an extra viewing is NZ$39. We tell you before we do it
  • Chat with our team until you move in

We cannot promise a particular room, because landlords decide. We promise a confirmed first stay, and honest help finding the rest.

Paid at cost

What you pay separately.

Your first stay

Paid to the hostel, homestay or motel at their price. You choose how many nights, from 3 to 28.

Your ride from the airport

A prepaid transfer at the price for your area, or Uber, or the bus. You choose.

Never your bond or rent

When you find a room, you pay your bond and rent straight to the landlord. They never pass through us.

Welcome pack

Ready to ride from day one.

Your fee includes your city’s bus card with $10 already on it, plus a NZ SIM.

  • $10 covers the airport bus into the city centre in Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch and Palmerston North.
  • Hamilton and Dunedin have no airport bus. Your card works on the city buses once you arrive.
  • In Christchurch you get a Motu Move card, which replaces the Metrocard.
  • Taking an Uber or a prepaid transfer instead of the bus? You still get the card, for the city buses once you arrive.

All prices are in New Zealand dollars and include GST. Stay and transfer prices are set by each provider and confirmed with you before you pay.
