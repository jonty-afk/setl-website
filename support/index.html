Support

How can we help?

Real people read every message. Write to us and we’ll reply as soon as we can.

Email hello@setl.nz

I didn’t get my sign-in code

Check your spam or junk folder, and make sure you typed your email correctly. Codes are sent from noreply@setl.nz and work for one hour. If it still doesn’t arrive after a few minutes, go back and request a new one, or email us from the same address.

How do I change my flight details?

Open the Arrival tab and tap Change. If your stay is already confirmed, the plan is locked so nothing changes by accident. Message the team in Chat and we’ll update it for you.

My stay isn’t confirmed yet. What does that mean?

We only show a stay as confirmed once it is really booked. While it says requested, we are booking it. You’ll see it change in the app, and we’ll message you the details.

How do I upload my documents?

Go to Profile and tap Add next to each document. You can upload a photo (JPG, PNG or HEIC) or a PDF, up to 10 MB each. Only you and our team can open them.

Can I change the language?

Yes. On the sign-in page tap the language button at the top, or open Profile and choose a language. The app works in English, हिंदी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ and 中文.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. You can also read how deletion works or email us from the address you signed up with.
