What SETL does

SETL helps you arrive in New Zealand (Auckland, Hamilton, Wellington, Palmerston North, Christchurch and Dunedin): airport meet and greet, a welcome pack, booking your first stay, and help finding a longer-term room. We arrange these services; the accommodation and transport themselves are provided by other businesses.

Your first stay

Your first stay is only confirmed when the app shows "First stay confirmed". Until then it is a request. If we cannot confirm a stay before you fly, we will refund our package fee.

Longer-term rooms

We help you find and check rooms, but we do not guarantee you a long-term room, and we are not your landlord. Your tenancy is between you and the landlord.

Payments

You pay our package fee (and any prepaid airport transfer) to us. You pay accommodation, rent and bond directly to the provider or landlord. We never hold your bond or rent.

Cancellations

Cancellation terms for your first stay are shown before you confirm. Our package fee is refundable [refund terms to be set] before your arrival.

Advice we do not give

We do not give immigration or visa advice. For that, talk to a licensed immigration adviser or a lawyer.

Your rights

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act or Fair Trading Act.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use SETL.
