Terms of service

The rules for using SETL, in plain language.

Last updated 23 November 2026. Questions about this page? Email hello@setl.nz.

What SETL does

SETL helps you arrive in New Zealand. We are open in Auckland and Hamilton, and we plan to open in more cities. In the cities we are not open in, we only give you free route information. In the open cities we offer airport meet and greet, a welcome pack, booking your first stay, and help finding a longer-term room. Your fee includes 2 listing checks and 1 room viewing, and we may offer more for an extra fee that we tell you before you pay. We cannot promise you a particular room, because landlords decide. We arrange these services; the accommodation and transport themselves are provided by other businesses.

Your first stay

Your first stay is only confirmed when the app shows "First stay confirmed". Until then it is a request. If we cannot confirm a stay before you fly, we will refund our package fee.

Longer-term rooms

We help you find and check rooms, but we do not guarantee you a long-term room, and we are not your landlord. Your tenancy is between you and the landlord.

Payments

You pay our package fee (and any prepaid airport transfer) to us. You pay accommodation, rent and bond directly to the provider or landlord. We never hold your bond or rent.

Cancellations and refunds

If you cancel, what we refund depends on how long before your arrival date you tell us (in New Zealand time). 14 days or more before: a full refund of our package fee and any prepaid transfer. 7 to 13 days before: half of them back. Less than 7 days before: no refund. Whenever we cannot confirm a first stay before you fly, you get a full refund, however late it is. Your first stay is paid to the host, so its cancellation terms are the host's; we show them to you before you confirm. To cancel, message us in the app or email hello@setl.nz. Refunds go back to the way you paid, within 10 working days.

Advice we do not give

We do not give immigration or visa advice. For that, talk to a licensed immigration adviser or a lawyer.

Your rights

Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the New Zealand Consumer Guarantees Act or Fair Trading Act.

Age

You must be 18 or older to use SETL.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as SETL grows. We show the date of the latest version at the top. If a change affects a booking you have already made, the terms you agreed to when you booked still apply to it.

New Zealand law

These terms are governed by New Zealand law, and New Zealand courts deal with any dispute. Please talk to us first at hello@setl.nz: most problems can be sorted quickly.
