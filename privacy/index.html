Privacy policy

What we collect, why, who sees it and how you stay in control.

Last updated 23 November 2026. Questions about this page? Email hello@setl.nz.

Who we are

SETL is based in Auckland, New Zealand. Its founders are Rishabh Sharma and Mili Sharma. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Contact our privacy officer at hello@setl.nz.

What we collect

Your name, email address, flight details, the choices you make in the app, the documents you upload for your renter pack (such as your passport, visa, enrolment letter and proof of funds), room listings you send us, and your chat messages with our team. If you join our early-access list on setl.nz, we keep your name, email, city and when you plan to fly, only to tell you when SETL opens. Ask us and we will remove you. If you ask for a video call on setl.nz, we keep your name, your WhatsApp number and the time you asked for, only to arrange the call. Ask us and we will remove them. If you ask to hear about SETL Jobs, we keep your name, email and city until jobs open or you delete your account. If you are an employer and join our employer list, we keep your business name, type of business, your name, phone number or email, city and the staff you need, only to contact you about SETL Jobs.

Why we collect it

To meet you at the airport, book your first stay, check your documents so we can give landlords a reference, view rooms for you, and talk to you. We only collect what we need for these purposes.

Your documents

Documents are sent over an encrypted connection and kept in private storage that only you and our team can open. Once we have checked a document, we delete the copy and keep only the result (for example "passport verified").

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed: your name and arrival details with the accommodation and transport you choose, and your verified renter reference (never the documents themselves) with landlords, if you ask us to. We never sell your information.

Services we use

To run SETL we use: Supabase (our database, sign-in and file storage), Resend (to send our emails), Zoho Mail (our team inbox), GitHub Pages (to host setl.nz), WhatsApp (only if you choose to message or video call us there) and a free exchange-rate service, open.er-api.com (only if you choose to see prices in a currency other than NZ dollars; your device asks it for the day’s rate and we send it no personal details). They process your information only to provide their service to us. When we start taking payments in the app, our payment provider will process your payment details; we never see or store your card number.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored with our technology provider, Supabase, on servers that may be outside New Zealand. We remain responsible for it under New Zealand law.

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you use SETL and for up to 12 months after your last activity, unless the law requires longer. You can delete your account yourself in the app (Profile, then Delete account) or email hello@setl.nz. When you delete it, we remove your account, uploaded documents, arrival plan and chat messages from SETL.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email hello@setl.nz. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the New Zealand Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

If something goes wrong

If a privacy breach is likely to cause serious harm, we will tell you and the Privacy Commissioner as soon as we can, as New Zealand law requires.
