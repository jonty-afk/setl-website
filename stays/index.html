First stays

Where you could stay.

Hostels, motels and homestay services near the main campuses. We book first nights in Auckland and Hamilton. The other cities are shown for information and are coming soon.

What to expect.

Three kinds of first stay. Read this before you choose, so nothing is a surprise.

Budget

Backpackers or hostel bed.

  • A bed in a shared room, usually 4 to 8 people.
  • Shared bathroom and kitchen. Bring a padlock for your locker.
  • The cheapest choice. Best if you don’t mind sharing.

Standard

Homestay or private room.

  • Your own room in a house, with a host family or flatmates.
  • Shared bathroom and kitchen.
  • Quieter than a hostel. Good for settling in.

Comfort

Motel.

  • Your own room with your own bathroom.
  • You don’t share a room with other guests.
  • The most private choice, and the most expensive.

Our team confirms your exact place, and you see the details in the app once it is booked. Photos and videos from our own visits will be added here.

Women-only stays.

Travelling alone, or a parent who worries? We can book a place where she stays with women only, or with a family.

  • A women-only dorm or a women-only floor in a hostel.
  • A private room of her own.
  • A homestay with a family.

We only offer places we have checked, and we tell you exactly what each one is before you confirm. A women-only dorm can be inside a hostel that also has men, so we say whether the bathroom, kitchen and entrance are shared. Ask in the app, or book a video call.

Each place sets its own prices, and they change with dates and demand. We confirm the exact price with you before you pay anything. SETL isn’t affiliated with these providers, and we can book other places too. Homestay services usually need 4 weeks or more and a few weeks’ notice, so for your first nights a hostel, guesthouse or motel is often easier.
