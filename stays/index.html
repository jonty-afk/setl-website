First stays

Where you could stay.

Hostels, motels and homestay services near the main campuses. We book first nights in Auckland and Hamilton. The other cities are shown for information and are coming soon.

What to expect.

Three kinds of first stay. Read this before you choose, so nothing is a surprise.

Budget

Backpackers or hostel bed.

  • A bed in a shared room, usually 4 to 8 people.
  • Shared bathroom and kitchen. Bring a padlock for your locker.
  • The cheapest choice. Best if you don’t mind sharing.

Standard

Homestay or private room.

  • Your own room in a house, with a host family or flatmates.
  • Shared bathroom and kitchen.
  • Quieter than a hostel. Good for settling in.

Comfort

Motel.

  • Your own room with your own bathroom.
  • You don’t share a room with other guests.
  • The most private choice, and the most expensive.

Our team confirms your exact place, and you see the details in the app once it is booked. Photos and videos from our own visits will be added here.

Prices are typical “from” rates from the provider’s own site or a booking site, checked in November 2026, in New Zealand dollars. They change with dates and demand, and the app shows your exact price before you confirm. SETL isn’t affiliated with these providers, and we can book other places too. Homestay services usually need 4 weeks or more and a few weeks’ notice, so for your first nights a hostel, guesthouse or motel is often easier.
