The app

Clear, calm and made for your first weeks.

One simple app for your plan, your rooms, your documents and your team. Coming soon to iPhone and Android.

Your plan, one step at a time

See what’s done and what’s next: your flight, your first stay and your renter pack. A boarding pass keeps your trip front and centre.

Rooms we’ve actually looked at

Paste a link from Trade Me, Facebook or anywhere else. We go and look, on video, and tick off the landlord, the photos and the bond before you decide.

Real people, in your language

Ask the team anything about your arrival, your stay or finding a room. Our people write back, and it’s never a chatbot.

A renter pack landlords trust

Upload your passport, visa, enrolment letter and proof of funds once. We check them and, only if you ask, share a verified reference with a landlord. Never the documents themselves.

Made for every newcomer

In your language, in light or dark.

Switch the app between English, हिंदी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ and 中文 whenever you like. It starts in your phone’s language when we offer it, and follows your phone’s light or dark setting.
