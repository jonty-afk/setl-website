Questions

Good questions, straight answers.

Do you guarantee me a specific flat?

No, and we won’t pretend to. We book your first stay and confirm it before you fly. If we can’t, we refund our fee. Finding a longer-term room happens with you, using video viewings and landlord checks.

Do you hold my money?

We never hold your bond or rent. You pay our package fee and, if you choose it, a prepaid airport transfer. Your stay is paid to the host. The app shows every amount before you confirm.
