From the airport

The cheapest way in, step by step.

Which bus, where it stops, what it costs and where to change. Pick the city you land in.

AKL

Auckland

From Auckland Airport. One orange bus, then one train.

Cost
About $6.50

Airport to the city centre, 3 fare zones

Bus runs
Every 10 minutes

4:30am to 12:40am, every day

Pay with
Bus card or bank card

No cash on buses

Luggage
Fine on board

The AirportLink has luggage space

Most students

To the city centre and west Auckland

  1. Auckland Airport

    International terminal: bus stop A, in the Transport Hub. Domestic terminal: bus stop B, outside door 4. Follow the orange signs.

  2. Bus: AirportLink bus

    The orange bus. Every 10 minutes, about 20 minutes to Puhinui.

  3. Puhinui Station

    Change to the train here. Tag off the bus, then tag on to the train within 30 minutes with the same card, and you pay one fare for the whole trip.

  4. Train: South City line or East West line

    Both lines go through the city centre.

  5. City centre

    Get off at Karanga-a-Hape, Te Waihorotiu or Waitematā station.

  6. Train: East West line only

    Stay on if you are going west.

  7. Maungawhau and west Auckland

    For New Lynn and Henderson. The most any trip costs is $7.90.

To Manukau and south Auckland

  1. Auckland Airport

    Same stops: A at international, B at domestic.

  2. Bus: AirportLink bus

    Stay on past Puhinui Station.

  3. Manukau Bus Station

    The last stop. For Papatoetoe, get off at Puhinui Station and take a train instead. Trips inside this zone cost $3.

To Māngere and Onehunga

  1. Auckland Airport

    Same stops as the AirportLink.

  2. Bus: Bus 38

    Every 15 to 20 minutes during the day. 30 to 35 minutes to Onehunga.

  3. Onehunga

    Goes through Māngere Town Centre on the way.

  4. Train: Onehunga West line

    If you are going further.

  5. Newmarket, Maungawhau and west Auckland

    Check your exact stop in the Journey Planner.

To your area

  • Auckland City

    CBD, Grafton

    AirportLink bus, then the train to a city centre station.

    About $6.50

  • South Auckland

    Papatoetoe, Manukau

    AirportLink bus to Manukau, or to Puhinui and one train to Papatoetoe.

    $3

  • West Auckland

    New Lynn, Henderson

    AirportLink bus, then stay on the East West line through the city.

    About $7.90

  • Central Auckland

    Mt Roskill, Sandringham

    This depends on your street. Put your address into the Journey Planner for the best bus.

    $7.90 at most

Good to know

  • Your SETL welcome pack has an AT HOP bus card with $10 on it, which covers this trip. You can also tap a contactless bank card or your phone.
  • Tag on when you get on and tag off when you get off, every time, with the same card.
  • Landing between 12:40am and 4:30am? The AirportLink is not running. Wait for the first bus, or take an Uber or a prepaid transfer.
  • Sometimes trains stop for track work and a rail replacement bus runs from Puhinui. It takes longer. The AT Mobile app shows this on the day.
  • If you need your own card, a machine at bus stop A sells AT HOP cards with $20 already loaded.

HLZ

Hamilton

From Hamilton Airport. There is no bus. Most students arrive through Auckland and take the coach.

Hamilton Airport has no public bus. If you land there, take a taxi or an Uber (the airport lists both). Most new students fly into Auckland and take the InterCity coach to Hamilton. That is the cheapest verified way, and the steps are below.

Coach from Auckland
From $20

InterCity, plus a small booking fee

Journey
About 1 hr 50

Auckland Airport to the Hamilton Transport Centre

Bags
2 bags, 25 kg each

Plus a carry-on, per person

City buses
About $3

With a Bee Card. Cash costs about double.

Cheapest

Coach from Auckland Airport to Hamilton

  1. Book online before you fly

    Book on the InterCity website. Choose the "Auckland Airport to Hamilton" coach.

  2. Flight: Land at Auckland Airport

    Collect your bags and go to the coach stop.

  3. Auckland Airport coach stop

    International terminal: stop B. Domestic terminal: stop D. Check the stop on your ticket.

  4. Coach: InterCity coach

    About 1 hour 50 minutes. Two bags of up to 25 kg each, plus a carry-on.

  5. Hamilton Transport Centre

    Corner of Bryce and Anglesea Streets, platforms A to D. This is the city bus station.

From the Transport Centre to the University of Waikato

Check the bus number in the BUSIT journey planner on the day.

  1. Hamilton Transport Centre

    Buy a Bee Card from the BUSIT counter (weekdays only) or the ticket shop here. Or bring cash.

  2. Bus: Bus 13 or 17 to the university

    Both leave from Platform Q. Bus 13 stops at Gate 1 on Knighton Road.

  3. University of Waikato, Gate 1

    The main gate on Knighton Road.

If you land at Hamilton Airport

Used by flights from Sydney and the Gold Coast.

  1. Hamilton Airport

    The taxi rank is at the main entrance. For Uber, follow the signs to the rideshare pick-up zone outside the arrivals lounge.

  2. Car: Taxi or Uber

    About 20 minutes to the city. Ask for the price before you start, or check it in the app.

  3. Your address

    The University of Waikato offers some students a free airport pickup. Ask about it at least 7 days before you arrive.

To your area

  • Hillcrest

    Near the University of Waikato

    Coach to the Transport Centre, then bus 13 or 17 to the university.

    From $20, then about $3

  • Hamilton Central

    City centre, Hamilton East

    Coach to the Transport Centre. It is in the city centre, so you can walk.

    From $20

  • Hamilton North

    Chartwell, Rototuna

    Coach to the Transport Centre, then bus 5 (Chartwell) or 16 (Rototuna).

    From $20, then about $3

Good to know

  • Book the coach before you fly, because cheap seats sell out.
  • Your SETL welcome pack has a bus card with $10 on it. Check with us which card yours is before you fly.
  • Hamilton buses take a Bee Card or cash only. Bank cards do not work yet. The ticket counter at the Transport Centre is closed at weekends, so bring coins.
  • Tell the driver your stop or raise your arm to stop a city bus, and tag off when you get off, or you pay a higher fare.
  • Landing late in Auckland? Check the last coach time on your ticket. If you miss it, take an Uber.
  • There is also a train (Te Huia) from Auckland, but it runs only a few times a day and costs $25.

WLG

Wellington SETL coming soon

From Wellington Airport. The Airport Express goes straight to the city. A cheaper bus needs a 7-minute walk.

Airport Express
$12

Or $9.23 if you pay with a Snapper card

Bus runs
Every 10 to 20 minutes

From about 5:30am to 10:25pm. Sundays start at 7:15am.

Pay with
Bank card, cash or Snapper

Tap once on the front reader if you pay by card

Luggage
Made for suitcases

The Airport Express has luggage racks. City buses may refuse big bags.

Easiest with suitcases

Airport Express to the city centre

  1. Wellington Airport

    Go out through Door E on the ground floor, and follow the "Airport Express" signs. The stop is less than 100 metres from the door, in the car park.

  2. Bus: Airport Express (AX)

    About 25 to 35 minutes to the city. It stops at Kilbirnie, Hataitai and Courtenay Place before the city centre.

  3. Courtenay Place

    Get off here for Te Aro. Further on it stops at Manners St, Willis St and Lambton Quay.

  4. Bus: Stay on

    If you are going to the main station.

  5. Wellington Station

    The last stop, at the Bus Interchange.

Cheaper

Route 2 bus to Te Aro and the city

It costs less than half as much, but there is a walk and it is harder with big bags.

  1. Wellington Airport

    Leave the terminal and walk to the bus stop called "Hobart Street at Broadway", stop 7033. The airport says it is 700 metres, about 7 minutes. Use a map on your phone to find it.

  2. Walk: Walk about 7 minutes

    Wave to the driver when your bus comes.

  3. Hobart Street at Broadway (stop 7033)

    Take the Route 2 bus. The front should say "2 Karori".

  4. Bus: Route 2

    It goes through Kilbirnie and Hataitai to Courtenay Place and Lambton Quay. About 20 minutes or more. Check the fare in the Metlink journey planner.

  5. Courtenay Place or Lambton Quay

    It does not go to the main station.

To Victoria University (Kelburn)

  1. Wellington Airport

    Take the Airport Express or the Route 2 bus.

  2. Bus: To Courtenay Place

    Get off at Courtenay Place, Stop A.

  3. Courtenay Place, Stop A

    Wait for the next bus on Route 21.

  4. Bus: Route 21, towards Kelburn

    About 8 minutes. Check the exact stop and time in the Metlink journey planner.

  5. Victoria University, Kelburn

    Get off at the stop for the Kelburn campus.

To your area

  • Wellington City

    Te Aro, Kelburn

    Airport Express to Courtenay Place. For Kelburn, change to Route 21.

    $12

  • Eastern suburbs

    Kilbirnie, Newtown

    Airport Express to Kilbirnie (5 minutes). For Newtown, change there to Route 3.

    $12

  • Northern suburbs

    Johnsonville, Karori

    Route 2 goes straight to Karori. For Johnsonville, go to Wellington Station and change to the train.

    Check the planner

Good to know

  • You can buy a Snapper card for $10 at the machine by Door E, near International Arrivals. The Airport Express costs less on a Snapper card, but for one trip a bank card is cheaper than buying the card.
  • Your SETL welcome pack has a bus card with $10 on it. Check with us which card yours is before you fly.
  • If you pay by bank card, it must be Visa or Mastercard. Tap on when you get on and tap off with the same card or phone on regular buses.
  • The last Airport Express is around 10:25pm, and on Saturdays there is no bus between 9:05pm and 10:20pm. The airport terminal closes from 1:30am to 3am.
  • Landing late? Take a taxi, an Uber or a prepaid transfer.

PMR

Palmerston North SETL coming soon

From Palmerston North Airport. A direct city bus, but the stop is a short walk from the terminal.

The bus stop at the terminal is closed for building work, until further notice. The bus stops on Airport Drive, about a 5-minute walk away. Allow extra time with suitcases, and ask the airport help desk if you are unsure which way to go.

Cost
About $2.20

One trip with a Bee Card. Cash costs $3.50.

Bus runs
Every 15 to 30 minutes

About 6:30am to 9pm, every day

Pay with
Bee Card or cash

Bring coins or small notes. Bank cards do not work yet.

Walk
About 5 minutes

From the terminal to the stop on Airport Drive

Most students

To the city centre and Massey University

  1. Palmerston North Airport

    Leave the terminal and walk to the bus stop on Airport Drive, about 5 minutes. The stop for the city is called "Airport, Outer Stop 3". Look for the sign.

  2. Walk: Walk to Airport Drive

    About 5 minutes. Wave to the driver when your bus comes, or it may not stop.

  3. Airport Drive, Outer Stop 3

    Take bus 101 (it says "Massey") or bus 302 (it says "Palmerston North").

  4. Bus: Bus 101 to Massey, or bus 302

    About 15 to 18 minutes to the city centre. Pay cash or tag on with your Bee Card.

  5. Main Street Bus Hub

    The city bus station near The Square. Tag off here if you are staying in the city.

  6. Bus: Stay on bus 101

    If you are going to Massey University, about 30 minutes in total.

  7. Massey University

    Stops along Fitzherbert Ave, then the campus on Tennent Drive.

To your area

  • City centre

    Central, Hokowhitu

    Bus 101 or 302 to the Main Street Bus Hub. For Hokowhitu, change there to bus 103.

    About $2.20

  • Fitzherbert

    Near Massey University

    Bus 101 direct. Stay on past the city centre.

    About $2.20

Good to know

  • You can buy a Bee Card from the driver with cash, or from the information centre in The Square. It costs $5, plus at least $5 of credit.
  • Your SETL welcome pack has a bus card with $10 on it. Check with us which card yours is before you fly.
  • Last bus from the airport is around 9pm, and the first is around 6:30am. If you land outside those hours, take a taxi or the Supershuttle, which the airport lists.
  • The stop will move back to the terminal when the building work is done. Until then, use Airport Drive.
  • Once you are enrolled, Massey and UCOL students ride free in Palmerston North with a registered Bee Card. It takes a couple of days to switch on.

CHC

Christchurch SETL coming soon

From Christchurch Airport. A direct bus to the university and the city. No change needed.

Cost
$3

One adult trip, any distance, with a bank card or Motu Move card

Bus runs
Several an hour

Early morning until about 10pm to 11:30pm

Pay with
Bank card or Motu Move

Metrocard and cash stop on 23 November 2026

Cap
$6 a day

You never pay more than $6 in one day, or $30 in a week

Most students

To the University of Canterbury and Riccarton

  1. Christchurch Airport

    Walk to the northern end of International Arrivals and go out through Door 9. The bus stop is next to the Novotel hotel. The walk is mostly under cover.

  2. Bus: Bus 3, towards Sumner

    Check the sign on the front says Sumner. About 15 minutes to the university and 20 to Riccarton.

  3. University of Canterbury, Ilam Rd

    Get off here for the campus and Ilam.

  4. Bus: Stay on bus 3

    If you are going to Riccarton.

  5. Westfield Riccarton

    The shopping centre. Buses to Lincoln leave from near here.

To the city centre

  1. Christchurch Airport

    Same stop: Door 9, next to the Novotel.

  2. Bus: Bus 3, 8 or 29

    Take whichever leaves first. About 30 minutes. Bus 29 goes through Fendalton and bus 8 carries on to Lyttelton.

  3. Bus Interchange

    The city bus station at Lichfield and Colombo Streets.

To Lincoln University

There is no direct bus. You change once.

  1. Christchurch Airport

    Same stop: Door 9, next to the Novotel.

  2. Bus: Bus 3, towards Sumner

    About 20 minutes to Riccarton.

  3. Westfield Riccarton

    Get off and change.

  4. Bus: Bus 80, towards Lincoln

    About 30 to 40 minutes. Check the exact stop and time in the Metro journey planner.

  5. Lincoln University

    Tag on both buses with the same card or phone, and the second ride is free.

To your area

  • Ilam and Riccarton

    Near the University of Canterbury

    Bus 3 direct, no change.

    $3

  • Christchurch Central

    City centre, Addington

    Bus 3, 8 or 29 to the Bus Interchange. For Addington, change there to a bus towards Halswell. Check the Journey Planner.

    $3

  • Lincoln

    Near Lincoln University

    Bus 3 to Westfield Riccarton, then bus 80.

    $3

Good to know

  • Your SETL welcome pack has a bus card with $10 on it. From 23 November 2026 Christchurch uses Motu Move cards, so we make sure yours is one.
  • You can also pay with a contactless Visa, Mastercard or American Express, or Apple Pay or Google Pay. Always use the same card or phone for each trip, and you can only pay for yourself.
  • Tag on at the front as you board and tag off when you get off. Two free changes within 2 hours.
  • Stand at the kerb and wave. If you do not, the driver may drive past.
  • The last buses leave around 10pm to 11:30pm. If you land later than that, take an Uber or a prepaid transfer.
  • Not sure your overseas bank card will work? Ask your bank before you fly, or ask us. A Motu Move card from a shop in the city is the backup.

DUD

Dunedin SETL coming soon

From Dunedin Airport. No bus here. A shared shuttle to your door is the cheapest way.

There is no public bus to Dunedin Airport. It is about 30 km from the city, so the cheapest way is a shared shuttle that takes you to your door. Book it before you fly.

Cost
About $25 to $35

Per person, shared, to North Dunedin. It depends on the company and your address.

Journey
30 minutes

The drive is about 20 to 30 minutes, longer with other stops

Book
Before you fly

Choose a company and give your flight number

Uber or taxi
About $90 to $100

The airport lists a $100 fixed-rate taxi

Cheapest

Shared shuttle to your door

Three shuttle companies serve the airport. Compare them on their own sites.

  1. Book online before you fly

    Give your flight number and your street address. If your flight is late, they move your pickup.

  2. Flight: Land at Dunedin Airport

    Collect your bags.

  3. Meet your driver

    Each company says how it meets you when you book. Check this, because it is different for each one.

  4. Shuttle: Shared shuttle

    Door to door. Other passengers may be dropped off first, so allow up to an hour.

  5. Your hall, flat or hotel

    North Dunedin, the University of Otago, the city centre and Roslyn are all served by address.

Taxi or Uber

Quicker with no other stops, but much more expensive.

  1. Dunedin Airport

    Taxis wait on the rank directly outside the main terminal building. For Uber, open the app and request a ride.

  2. Car: Taxi or Uber

    About 20 minutes. One taxi company offers a $100 fixed rate to the city.

  3. Your address

    For two or more students sharing the cost, this can be fair value.

To your area

  • North Dunedin

    Near the University of Otago

    Shared shuttle to your door. Put your hall or flat address in the booking.

    About $25 to $35

  • Dunedin Central

    City centre, Roslyn

    Shared shuttle to your door, same companies.

    About $25 to $35

Good to know

  • Book your shuttle before you fly. The airport itself recommends this, and walk-up seats are not guaranteed.
  • Compare bag allowances. Some companies include two suitcases and a carry-on, and some include only one suitcase and charge for the next.
  • Ask each company if it has a student price, and check if your university has a discount code.
  • Your SETL welcome pack has a bus card with $10 on it. It works for the city buses once you arrive.
  • Prefer us to book it? Choose the prepaid transfer in the app and we arrange it for a fixed price.
