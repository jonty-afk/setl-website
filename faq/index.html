About SETL

Who is SETL for?

International students and other newcomers arriving anywhere in New Zealand, who want a calm first week instead of a stressful one. We cover Auckland, Hamilton, Wellington, Palmerston North, Christchurch and Dunedin. You need to be 18 or older.

When can I get the app?

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. Join the early-access list and we’ll email you the moment they’re ready.

Which languages does the app use?

English, हिंदी (Hindi), ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi) and 中文 (Chinese). You can switch at any time.

Your stay and your ride

Do you guarantee me a specific flat?

No, and we won’t pretend to. We book your first stay and confirm it before you fly. If we can’t, we refund our fee. Finding a longer-term room happens with you, using video viewings and landlord checks.

What if my flight is delayed?

We check your flight before you land and adjust your pickup if it’s delayed, so nobody is left waiting.

What if I’d rather book my own ride?

Choose “Your own Uber”. You pay the driver, and we help you book it at arrivals. There’s also a bus and train guide if you’d like the cheapest way in.

Can I change my plan after my stay is confirmed?

Once a stay is confirmed, the plan is locked so nothing changes by accident. Message the team in the app and we’ll update it for you.

Money

Do you hold my money?

We never hold your bond or rent. You pay our package fee and, if you choose it, a prepaid airport transfer. Your stay is paid to the host. The app shows every amount before you confirm.

What does SETL cost?

A fixed package fee, plus your stay (paid to the host) and your ride (fixed for prepaid transfers). We’re finalising launch prices, and you’ll always see them in the app before you confirm anything.

Your information

Is my information safe?

Your files are kept in private storage, and each person can only see their own information. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Read our privacy policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, any time, in the app: Profile, then Delete account. It removes your account, documents, plan and chat. Here’s how it works.
