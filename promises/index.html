Our promises

Promises we keep.

You’re trusting us with your first days in a new country. Here’s exactly what you can count on.

We never hold your bond or rent

Your bond and rent never pass through us. We only take our package fee and, if you choose it, your prepaid transfer.

“Confirmed” means booked

We only mark your first stay as confirmed once it is really booked. Until then, we’ll say so.

A refund if we can’t confirm a stay

If we can’t confirm a stay for you before you fly, we refund our fee.

Licensed drivers only

Every prepaid transfer is driven by a licensed driver, at the price for your area you see before you pay.

Your documents stay private

Only you and our team can open them. Once we’ve checked one, we delete the copy and keep only the result.

Delete your account any time

In the app, in a couple of taps. It removes your account, documents, plan and chat from SETL.

Just as important

Lines we won’t cross.

Trust is also about being straight with you.

×

We won’t promise you a specific flat

Nobody can honestly promise a particular flat before you arrive. We promise a confirmed first stay, and honest help finding the rest with video viewings and landlord checks.

×

We won’t call something confirmed until it is

You’ll never see “confirmed” on a booking we haven’t actually made.

×

We won’t sell your information

We share only what’s needed for your booking, such as your name with the host or driver.

Your information

Handled with care.

Private storage

Your documents are kept in a private store that isn’t open to the public.

Only your own

The system is built so you can only ever see your own information.

NZ Privacy Act

We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

You’re in control

Delete your account yourself, whenever you like.
