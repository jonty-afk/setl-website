What you get

Everything for your first weeks, in one place.

From the moment you land to the day you find a room you love.

Meet and greet

A real person with your name on a sign at arrivals.

Welcome pack

A local transport card and a SIM, handed to you when you land.

Prepaid transfer

A licensed driver takes you to your stay, at a set price for your area that you see before you pay.

A first stay

A hostel bed, homestay or motel for 3 to 28 nights while you find your feet, confirmed only once it’s really booked.

Rooms viewed for you

Send us a listing. We view it on video and check the landlord, the photos and the bond. Your fee includes 2 listing checks and 1 viewing.

A renter pack

Upload your documents once. We check them so landlords can trust you.

Real people

Chat with our team in the app: until you move in. Never a chatbot.

Your language

The app speaks English, हिंदी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ and 中文.

Your first stay

Pick the kind of place that suits you.

Choose how long you need it, from a few days up to four weeks. Your stay is paid to the host, and we confirm it in the app once it’s really booked.

Budget

Hostel or backpackers

A bed in a shared room. The simplest way to land, and good for meeting people.

Standard

Homestay or private room

Your own room in a home, with a bit more space and quiet to settle in.

Comfort

Motel with your own bathroom

Your own space and bathroom, for when you want comfort straight after a long flight.

Getting to your stay

Three ways to get there.

Paid before you fly

Prepaid transfer

We book a licensed driver for you. You see the price for your area before you pay, and pay before you fly, so there’s nothing to pay at the airport.

You pay the driver

Your own Uber

Prefer to book your own? We help you set it up at arrivals, and you pay the driver.

Cheapest way in

Bus and train guide

A step-by-step route from the airport to your stay. In cities with no airport bus, we point you to the cheapest way in. The route guide is free in every city, even where we are not open yet.
