Your account

Delete your SETL account

You’re in control of your information. Here is how to delete your account and what happens when you do.

In the app

  1. Open the SETL app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Read the message and tap Delete my account to confirm.

The deletion happens straight away and you are signed out.

What we delete

  • Your SETL account and sign-in
  • The documents you uploaded for your renter pack
  • Your arrival plan and flight details
  • Your room viewing requests
  • Your chat messages

Before you delete

Deleting your account does not cancel a stay or ride that we have already booked for you. If you need to cancel one, message the team in Chat first.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you come back later, you will need to start again with a new account.

Can’t get into the app?

Email hello@setl.nz from the email address you signed up with, and ask us to delete your account. We’ll confirm when it’s done.

More about how we handle your information is in our privacy policy.
